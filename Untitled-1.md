
Ratcliff & McKoon (2008) describe chi-sq ddm-fitting method, which:
- breaks RTs down into quantiles (.1,.3,.5,.7,.9)
- ddm predicts cumulative prob of correct response in each RT quantile
- subtract the cumulative prob for each successive quantile from the next higher quantile to get the proportion of responses b/w adjacent quantiles
- the previous step becomes the expected values for chi-sq test
